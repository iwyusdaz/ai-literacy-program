What is happening here

Every time you press “Generate next word”, the tiny model looks at the sentence so far and picks one word from a probability list. Nothing checks whether the sentence is true — that is not part of the machinery. The temperature slider reshapes the list: at low temperature the most likely word dominates; at high temperature, unlikely words get picked more often, so sentences become creative and sometimes nonsense.

Real chatbots work the same way in principle — with vastly bigger lists and patterns learned from enormous amounts of text. The key consequence is the same: fluent is not the same as correct. A chatbot can produce a beautiful sentence about something entirely false, with the same confident tone. That is why checking outputs is a skill of its own — see Workplace Module 2 or School Lesson 2.
