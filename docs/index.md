# AI Literacy Program

{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }
A modular, risk-aware AI literacy curriculum. It teaches **verification, data judgment, bias awareness and human oversight** — not prompt tricks.

Two tracks built on the same core concepts:

| Track | For | Time | Start |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Workplace track** | Employees who use AI tools at work (customer service, operations, HR) | 4 self-paced modules, ~20 min each, plus a 1-page job aid | [Start the workplace track](workplace/index.html) |
| **School track** | Students aged 14–16, taught by a non-specialist teacher | 4 lessons × 50 min + final project | [Start the school track](school/index.html) |

## What this program is

Many organizations must now take measures to support staff AI literacy under [Article 4 of the EU AI Act](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-4). Schools have the [OECD/EC AILit framework](https://ailiteracyframework.org). Most existing training focuses on productivity. This program is different: it treats AI literacy as a set of **judgments people make in real tasks** — Can I trust this output? May I paste this data? Who is affected if I act on it? When should a human decide?

The program is mapped to the final AILit framework domains and competences and designed to support Article 4 AI literacy measures. It is **not** legal advice, and it does not certify anyone as "AI Act compliant" — Article 4 does not require any individual to reach a specific literacy level.
