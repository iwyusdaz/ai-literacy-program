# Job aid: Verify before you send

**One page. Five checks. Use for any text you did not fully write yourself.**

*Last reviewed: 2026-10-25*

> **Before you send, say each line and mean it:**

## 1. The claim

**Is every statement true — or only plausible?**
Anything you cannot confirm from a system or a document is a maybe. A promise ("within 48 hours"), a policy, a price, a status — check it or cut it.

## 2. The numbers

**Check every figure in the source.**
AI does not calculate; it predicts number-shaped text. Totals, percentages, dates, amounts — every one, against the original. One wrong number in a confident table is invisible.

## 3. The source

**Does the cited source exist, and does it say this?**
Open the link. Find the paragraph. No source you would show your manager = no source. Invented organizations and reports look completely normal.

## 4. The data

**Did I paste anything that must not leave the company?**
Names, addresses, order numbers linked to a person, card details, complaint texts, CVs — prohibited. Internal figures — approved tools only. Already pasted something prohibited? Stop, report today. Never hide it.

## 5. Who is affected

**Does acting on this change something for a person?**
Their money, job, health, safety, rights, reputation → **a human decides**. AI may inform. Name the person who owns the decision. Unclear or pressured → escalate (lead / DPO).

---

**If any check fails:** fix it, or stop and escalate. Sending is not the goal; standing behind it is.

**Remember the pattern from the modules:**

| Situation | Your move |
|---|---|
| Draft reads fluently | That feeling is not evidence — run the five checks |
| Number appears in a summary | Verify it in the source before repeating it |
| Cited source you've never heard of | Open it, or delete the claim |
| Customer data in your prompt | Remove it; redraft with a description, not an identity |
| Decision about a person | Human decides; AI informs |
