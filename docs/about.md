# About this program

## Purpose

This program helps two audiences build practical, risk-aware AI literacy:

1. **Employees** who use AI tools in their daily work and need to make good judgment calls — about verification, data, bias and oversight.
2. **Students aged 14–16**, who are growing up with AI and need to understand how it works, where it fails, and when it helps or harms learning.

It was designed to support organizations' Article 4 (EU AI Act) AI literacy measures and schools' use of the OECD/EC AILit framework. It is **not legal advice** and it does not certify anyone. Article 4 requires providers and deployers to *take measures* to support AI literacy; it explicitly does not require any individual to reach a specific level of literacy.

## How to adapt it

All learning content is CC BY 4.0. You can reuse, translate and adapt it with attribution. Practical notes:

- **Modules are modular.** Each workplace module stands alone. You can deliver them in any order, though 1 → 4 is the intended sequence.
- **Swap the organization.** Northwind Logistics is fictional. Replace its examples with your own organization's scenarios, data rules and escalation contacts.
- **Reading level.** Workplace text targets B1 plain English (about grade 8) for multilingual workforces. Keep that level when you adapt.
- **The job aid is the takeaway.** Adapt [Verify before you send](workplace/job-aid-verify-before-you-send.html) to your tools and policies — that single page often matters more than the modules.
- **School lessons need no devices.** Every lesson has an unplugged fallback in the [teacher guide](school/teacher-guide.html).
