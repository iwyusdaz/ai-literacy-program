## The answer you will check

Read it once, normally. Mark each sentence: **T** (sounds true), **?** (not sure), **F** (sounds false). *Then* start checking.

> "The Great Wall of China is the only man-made structure visible from space with the naked eye. It stretches exactly 21,196 kilometers. The wall was built mainly to keep out rabbits and deer, a fact documented in the 1998 book *Walls of the Middle Kingdom* by Professor Henry Caldwell."

## Step 2 — what kind of claim is it?

Claims with a **number, date, name, source, or superlative** ("the only", "exactly") always get checked. Underline every claim type in the text above.

## Teacher's answer key (for reference)

The "visible from space" claim is a well-known **myth** (astronauts have said it is not visible to the naked eye). The **21,196 km** figure is roughly correct for all sections of the wall across dynasties — students should notice it needs a *named* source to trust. The **rabbit/deer** purpose is **invented**. The **1998 book** does **not exist** — a fabricated source. Objective met with **two verified claims**; the class discussion about why the false ones sounded fine is the lesson.
