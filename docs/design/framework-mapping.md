# Framework mapping

Mapped against the **final** OECD/EC AILit framework — *Empowering Learners for the Age of AI* (June 2026), domains **Engage with AI / Create with AI / Manage AI / Shape AI** ([ailiteracyframework.org](https://ailiteracyframework.org), accessed 2026-10-25; see [research notes](https://github.com/iwyusdaz/ai-literacy-program/blob/main/research/framework-notes.md)). The June 2025 draft named the fourth domain "Designing AI"; the final version renamed it "Shape AI". Competence names below are quoted from the final framework.

AILit is written for primary and secondary education. It applies **directly** to the school track; the workplace track uses the same domains as a reference structure (scenarios differ). Article 4 considerations apply to the workplace track.

*Last reviewed: 2026-10-25*

## Honest gaps and why

1. **Shape AI (all 4 competences) is out of scope** — designing, evaluating and improving AI systems belongs to a different program (and AILit's Advanced progression level). A user-literacy program that pretends to cover "design AI systems" would overclaim.
2. **Engage 5 (energy) and Manage 2 (choosing between AI approaches)** are omitted for time: 80 minutes cannot cover them with practice and assessment. If the program expands, they are the first additions.
3. **Create with AI is light** — this program is deliberately risk-focused (its differentiator), not a creativity course. Lesson 4 shows the constructive use case (AI as feedback partner) without pretending to teach prompt craft.
4. **AILit progression levels**: this program targets the **Basic** level across the board, with touches of Intermediate in Lesson 2 and Modules 2–4. No Advanced-level outcomes are claimed.

## Article 4 considerations (workplace track)

Article 4 (as amended, consolidated 27 July 2026) requires providers and deployers to **take measures** to support AI literacy of staff and others operating AI on their behalf, tailored to knowledge, training, context of use and affected persons — and explicitly does **not** require any individual to reach a specific level. Mapping:

| Article 4 element | Program response |
|---|---|
| Measures proportionate to knowledge/training | Modules assume zero prior knowledge; B1 plain English |
| Context of use | All scenarios from customer service, ops planning, HR |
| Persons or groups affected | Module 4's affected-person test (W4.1, W4.2) |
| Staff **and** others operating on the deployer's behalf | Content is role-agnostic; job aid usable by contractors |
| No required level/format/certificate | Program targets observable decisions (LTEM Tier 5), not seat time; no certificate is claimed |

The program is **designed to support** Article 4 measures. It is not legal advice and certifies nothing.
