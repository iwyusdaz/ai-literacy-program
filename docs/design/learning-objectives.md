# Learning objectives

Each objective states **condition → behavior → criterion** and uses an observable verb. Every objective appears in the [alignment matrix](alignment-matrix.html) and is assessed. Objectives were drafted with AI assistance and reviewed during the build; the structured review of objectives is documented in [evaluation report](evaluation-report.html).

*Last reviewed: 2026-10-25*

## Workplace track (W)

### Module 1 — What AI is (and isn't)

- **W1.1** Given a short description of how an AI assistant produced an answer, **the learner identifies** the explanation as "predicting likely text" and **corrects** the misconception that the system "looked it up" or "knows facts", **identifying at least two of the three misconceptions listed in the module**.
- **W1.2** Given eight work tasks from a logistics context, **the learner classifies** each as a good fit, a risky fit, or a poor fit for generative AI, **with a correct classification on at least six of eight**.

### Module 2 — Checking outputs

- **W2.1** Given an AI-drafted customer reply that contains one factual error and one unverifiable claim, **the learner identifies both** and **names the source needed to verify or correct each**, **correctly on both counts**.
- **W2.2** Given an AI-drafted reply and the [verification checklist](../workplace/job-aid-verify-before-you-send.html), **the learner applies** the checklist and **produces a corrected reply and a completed verification log**, **with all five checks addressed**.

### Module 3 — Data and privacy

- **W3.1** Given eight items of information that could be pasted into an AI tool (names, order numbers, anonymized totals, internal pricing, etc.), **the learner classifies** each as permitted / restricted / prohibited under the (fictional) Northwind data rule, **correctly on at least seven of eight**.
- **W3.2** Given the scenario "you already pasted customer data into an AI tool", **the learner selects** the correct containment and reporting steps in the correct order, **per the escalation path taught in the module**.

### Module 4 — Oversight and escalation

- **W4.1** Given four scenarios where AI suggests an action that affects a person (customer compensation, CV ranking, route planning affecting drivers, discipline), **the learner decides** for each whether a human must make the decision and **identifies** the correct escalation contact, **correctly on at least three of four**.
- **W4.2** Given a proposed AI use, **the learner names** at least two signals that mark it as high-risk (decisions about people, safety, legal effect; bias potential; scale), **with at least two correct signals**.

## School track (S) — ages 14–16

### Lesson 1 — How AI makes text

- **S1.1** After using the [next-word interactive](../interactives/next-word/index.html), **the student describes** in their own words how a chatbot produces text (it predicts a likely next word), **correcting the misconception that it "knows" or "looks things up"**.

### Lesson 2 — When AI gets it wrong

- **S2.1** Given a chatbot answer to a history question, **the student identifies at least two claims that need checking** and **verifies each with a reliable source**, **recording both on the [fact-check worksheet](../school/handouts/fact-check-worksheet.html)**.

### Lesson 3 — Bias and fairness

- **S3.1** Given two AI outputs describing the same role or situation, **the student identifies** one way the outputs could be unfair to a group and **explains** how training data or output choices could cause it, **with a cause that matches the example**.

### Lesson 4 — Using AI to learn, not to skip

- **S4.1** Given four homework scenarios, **the student decides** for each whether AI use would help learning or replace thinking, and **writes** two personal AI-use rules in the [handout](../school/handouts/my-ai-use-rules.html), **with reasons for each rule**.

## Assessment coverage

- Scenario quiz: 12 items → W1.1, W1.2, W2.1, W2.2, W3.1, W3.2, W4.1, W4.2 ([items mapping](alignment-matrix.html))
- Performance task (both tracks): W2.2, W3.1, W4.1 (workplace variant) / S2.1, S3.1 (school variant), scored with the [rubric](../assessment/rubric.html)
- Final project (school): S1.1–S4.1 applied together
