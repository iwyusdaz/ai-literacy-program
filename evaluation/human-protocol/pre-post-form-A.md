# Pre-test — Form A (6 scenario items)

*Given BEFORE Modules 2–3. Read the situation, pick the best answer. No notes, no tools. About 10 minutes. Circle one letter per item. Scoring key at the end (facilitator only). Parallel form of Form B — do not use both for the same participant on the same day.*

Participant code: ______ · Date: ______

---

**A1.** A colleague says: "The assistant gave me the exact delivery date, so it must have looked it up in our order system." The most accurate response is:

- **a)** "Yes — it must be connected to our systems."
- **b)** "It predicted a likely-sounding date. Unless a tool is connected, it can't see our systems — check it in the order system first."
- **c)** "The AI was trained on shipping data, so its dates are reliable."
- **d)** "It doesn't matter for a quick answer."

**A2.** An AI-drafted reply says: "As discussed last Tuesday, your replacement parcel arrives tomorrow before noon." You have no record of a conversation last Tuesday and have not checked stock. Before sending you should:

- **a)** Send it — it sounds professional.
- **b)** Remove "as discussed last Tuesday", check the delivery date against the system, and fix or remove the promise.
- **c)** Shorten the sentence to make it friendlier.
- **d)** Add "please note dates may vary" at the end.

**A3.** An AI memo cites "the 2023 European Parcel Performance Report by the European Logistics Board". You have never heard of either. You should:

- **a)** Keep it — official-sounding sources are safe.
- **b)** Ask the AI to confirm the report exists.
- **c)** Check whether the report and organization exist and say this; if you can't verify, remove the citation and the claim.
- **d)** Swap in a more famous report name.

**A4.** You want a good draft reply. Which prompt follows the data rule taught in this program?

- **a)** "Draft a reply for Mrs. Petra Weber, order NW-88123, Musterstraße 12, about her late delivery."
- **b)** "Draft a friendly reply to a customer whose delivery is late; apologize and promise a tracking update today."
- **c)** "Here is the customer's full email — write what she wants to hear."
- **d)** "Use this customer's complaint text and her phone number as context."

**A5.** You already pasted a customer's name and address into a personal chatbot account yesterday. Today you should first:

- **a)** Delete the chat and say nothing.
- **b)** Wait and see whether the customer complains.
- **c)** Stop using that chat and report it today so your lead and the data protection officer can decide next steps.
- **d)** Paste the same data into the approved tool instead.

**A6.** HR proposes AI-scoring 240 CVs and inviting the top 20 automatically. The best call is:

- **a)** Fine — humans send the invitations afterwards.
- **b)** Fine — the model has seen millions of CVs, so it's objective.
- **c)** AI may inform, but scoring and rejecting people must stay a human decision; any AI screening needs a formal decision with the data protection officer and HR leadership.
- **d)** No AI may ever be used in HR at all.
