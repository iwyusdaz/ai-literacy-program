## Participant information and consent

**What this is.** You are invited to try out parts of a training program about using AI at work, and to tell us what you found confusing. This is a small usability and learning pilot for a portfolio project.

**What you will do (about 60 minutes total):**
1. A short pre-test: 6 scenario questions (about 10 minutes).
2. Two self-paced online modules, each about 20 minutes.
3. A short post-test: 6 scenario questions (about 10 minutes).
4. One practical task: correct a short flawed document and note your checks (about 15 minutes).
5. A 5-question survey about your experience.
Optionally, during parts of the session you may be asked to think aloud (say what you are looking at and what you are thinking).

**Voluntary.** Taking part is entirely voluntary. You can skip any question, take a break, or stop at any time without giving a reason and without any consequence.

**Your data and privacy.**
- We do not collect your name. You get a participant code (P1, P2, …); results never connect answers to an identity.
- Session notes record what you did and said about the materials — not personal information.
- The website itself stores nothing: the quiz and interactive send nothing anywhere.
- If the session is on a call, we will ask before recording; recordings (if any) are used only to write notes and are deleted after the notes are made.
- Anonymized quotes from your feedback may appear in the public project write-up, with nothing that could identify you.

**No risks beyond everyday computer use are expected.** The materials use a fictional company (Northwind Logistics) and fictional scenarios; no real customer or personal data appears in them. Taking part gives you no employment benefit and costs you nothing.

**Compensation.** None, unless separately agreed in writing by the organizer.

**Questions or withdrawal.** Contact the organizer at any point before, during, or after the session: [Email — owner to fill in]. If you withdraw, your data is deleted on request.

**Consent statement.** By replying "I consent" (in writing, email or message) you confirm: you are 18 or older; you have read this information; you agree to take part; you agree that anonymized quotes may be used.

*Record for each participant: date, participant code, "I consent" received (Y/N), think-aloud (Y/N). No other personal data is collected.*
