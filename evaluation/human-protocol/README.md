## Ground rules

1. **Informed consent** before any session; withdraw at any time without reason.
2. **Collect no names** in the data; use participant codes (P1, P2…).
3. **No minors** under this consent text. Any school-track testing with students requires guardian consent and institutional approval first — do not reuse these forms for that.
4. **Label results by method**: human pilot results are human results; anything from the pipelines is labeled "AI-simulated" or "Agent review".
5. Report honestly: small n, no significance claims, confusions are findings not failures.
