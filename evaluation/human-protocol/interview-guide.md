# Interview guide — 15 minutes (optional input for needs analysis)

*Status in this build: NOT RUN. The needs analysis used published secondary research instead (`research/secondary-research.md`). This guide is kept ready so the owner can collect first-hand input later. Use the consent sentence below verbatim; anonymize everything.*

---

## Consent sentence (read before starting)

> "This is a 15-minute chat about how you use AI tools at work. I won't record your name, nothing you say will be connected to you, and you can stop any time. Anonymized notes may be used in a public write-up. OK to continue?"

## Warm-up (2 min)

1. "In a normal week, which tasks does AI touch in your work — if any?"
2. "Which tool(s), and for roughly what share of those tasks?"

## Current practice (5 min)

3. "Walk me through the last time you asked an AI tool for help with a work task. What did you paste in, and what did you do with the output?"
4. "Has an AI output ever been wrong on something that mattered? What happened?"
5. "Is there anything you would not paste into an AI tool? How do you decide?"

## Judgment and escalation (4 min)

6. "If an AI suggestion affected a customer or a colleague — say a refund, or ranking job applicants — where is the line for you between 'the AI says' and 'I decide'?"
7. "If you realized you had pasted something you shouldn't have, what would you actually do?"

## Wrap-up (4 min)

8. "If your employer offered 40 minutes of AI training, what should it absolutely cover? What should it skip?"
9. "Anything I should have asked but didn't?"

## Note-taking template (per participant code, no names)

- Role family (customer service / ops / HR / other), experience band
- Tasks where AI is used, tools (categories, not brand endorsements)
- Verification behavior observed/described (none / spot checks / routine)
- Data behavior described
- Escalation knowledge (knows a path Y/N)
- Direct quotes worth anonymizing
- Anything the participant could not answer
