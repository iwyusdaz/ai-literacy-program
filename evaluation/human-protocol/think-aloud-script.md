# Think-aloud script (interactive + quiz, 15 minutes)

*For 2–3 pilot participants, after the post-test. The facilitator reads the script lines verbatim where quoted. Record observations with timestamps against participant code — no names.*

---

## Setup (1 min)

> "For the next 15 minutes I'll ask you to use two things from the training while saying out loud whatever comes into your head — what you're looking at, what you're trying to do, what confuses you. There are no wrong actions; the materials are being tested, not you. I won't help unless you're truly stuck, and I'll note when that happens."

Confirm consent for note-taking. Open the interactive: `/interactives/next-word/`.

## Task 1 — Next-word interactive (7 min)

> "This is a toy version of how a chatbot picks words. Task: make one full sentence, then make the same starter give you a more surprising word. Say everything you're thinking."

**Facilitator notes to watch for (tick when observed):**

- [ ] Finds "Generate next word" without help (count hints needed: ___)
- [ ] Understands the probability table (quote their reading of it)
- [ ] Discovers what the temperature slider does — by themselves / after trying / never
- [ ] Connects the interactive to the "it doesn't check truth" idea unprompted (Y/N)
- [ ] Any keyboard or focus confusion
- [ ] Confusion points (verbatim): ________________

**Probe if silent (use at most once each):**
- "What are you looking at now?"
- "What do you expect to happen if you press that?"

## Task 2 — Scenario quiz (7 min)

> "Now the practice quiz — 12 short scenarios. Answer as you would at work. Again: think aloud."

Open `/assessment/scenario-quiz/`. Let them answer at least 5 items.

**Facilitator notes to watch for:**

- [ ] Reads the feedback after answering (Y/N) — or skips it
- [ ] Any item where they hesitate or re-read the stem (note item number: ___)
- [ ] Any option they call "unclear wording" or "two right answers" (note: ___)
- [ ] Understands the results-by-objective table (Y/N)
- [ ] Confusion points (verbatim): ________________

## Wrap-up (1 min)

> "Two quick questions: What was the most confusing moment? If you could change one thing, what?"

Record answers verbatim. Thank them.

## After the session

- Transfer ticks and quotes to the evaluation log within 24 hours.
- Confusion points found by 2+ participants go straight to the revision list (see the decision thresholds in the evaluation plan).
